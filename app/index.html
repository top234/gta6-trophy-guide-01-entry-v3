    How to use this guide

    1. Follow the Roadmap in order — story first, missables before the finale, cleanup last.
    2. Check trophies off as you earn them; progress saves in your browser and the ring, stats and roadmap all update.
    3. Open any trophy card for its full step-by-step guide, tip and sources.
    4. Export your progress to a file as a backup, and import it back any time.

    Missable Trophies — don’t get locked out

    Seven trophies can pass you by: partner-gated moments, a rival who may leave, story choices that lock in, and one-off events. All seven must be earned before the final mission. Missable flags are read from the official descriptions; the game may let you replay chapters, but don’t count on it.
